Privacy & sources

TALAL MUSIC is a personal, non-commercial player. Searches are sent through Cloudflare to the selected provider. A short-lived, hashed network identifier is used to enforce daily request limits. Local files and saved lists remain in your browser, not synced between devices. Online artwork loads from the provider. No Google sign-in is requested.
